Tools

Small utilities for internal use. Each one runs in your browser; nothing you type is uploaded.

QR note encoder

Type a note, make a printable QR code holding up to about 4,300 characters, then scan it later to read the text back. Uppercase letters, digits, spaces, and basic punctuation only.

Encrypted QR

Encrypt a note with a key you generate or one you already use. You get two QR codes: the key and the encrypted note. Both are needed to read it, and any text works, not just capital letters.
